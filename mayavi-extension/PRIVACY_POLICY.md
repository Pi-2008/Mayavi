# Privacy Policy — Character Summoner

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Character Summoner ("the extension") lets you summon an animated character
overlay on webpages by speaking a trigger phrase.

## What the extension accesses
- **Microphone audio**: only while the listening window is open and you have
  granted microphone permission. Audio is sent to Google's built-in Chrome
  Speech Recognition service (the browser's own `SpeechRecognition` API) to
  convert speech to text. The extension developer does not receive, log, or
  store this audio anywhere.
- **Webpage content**: the extension injects a small script into the active
  tab only to display the character video overlay. It does not read, collect,
  or transmit the content of any webpage you visit.
- **New Tab page**: the extension replaces Chrome's New Tab page with
  https://www.google.com so the character can also appear there. No data
  about your browsing is collected in the process.

## What is NOT collected
The extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data,
browsing history, audio recordings, or webpage content to the developer or
any third party other than Google's built-in speech recognition service
described above, which is part of the Chrome browser itself.

## Data retention
No data is retained by this extension. Audio is processed transiently by
Chrome's speech service and not stored by the extension.
